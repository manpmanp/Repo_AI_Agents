What we offer

A small set of assistants, each built to do one job well.

We'd rather do one thing properly than promise everything at once. Here's what we currently build — each one starts as a focused pilot before it grows into anything bigger.

Customer Service

AI Receptionist & Chat Assistant

Answers calls and website chats, handles common questions, and books appointments — so customers get a fast reply even when no one's free to answer.

  • Website chat and WhatsApp support
  • FAQ answers, around the clock
  • Hands off to a real person when needed
Scheduling

Booking & Scheduling Assistant

Manages your calendar, sends reminders, and reduces no-shows — without you needing to check a booking app all day.

  • Automatic appointment reminders
  • Conflict-free calendar management
  • Easy rescheduling for customers
Administration

Admin & Invoice Assistant

Handles the paperwork side of the business — reading documents, creating invoices, and chasing late payments politely and consistently.

  • Invoice creation and tracking
  • Payment reminders that don't get forgotten
  • Less manual data entry
Sales

Lead Follow-Up Assistant

Keeps track of new inquiries and makes sure none of them go cold — automatic, well-timed follow-ups instead of relying on memory.

  • Reminders for warm leads
  • Simple re-engagement messages
  • A clear view of who you've already contacted
Custom

A Custom Assistant, Built Around Your Workflow

If none of the above quite fits, we start from your actual day-to-day process and design something narrower and more specific to what you need. This is usually where the strongest results come from — a small, well-targeted assistant beats a generic one every time.

Not sure which one fits your business?

Tell us a bit about how things currently work, and we'll suggest a sensible starting point.
