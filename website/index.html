Common questions

Do I need any technical knowledge to work with you?

No. You explain how your business runs today, and we handle the technical side — including a plain-language walkthrough before anything goes live.

How long before I see something working?

A first working pilot typically takes a few weeks, not months, because we start with one focused assistant rather than a full platform.

What if it doesn't work out?

We'd rather find that out early and honestly than string out a project that isn't delivering. The first engagement is scoped small on purpose.

Is my customer data handled responsibly?

Yes — data handling and legal basis are agreed upfront, in plain language, before any customer information is touched.
