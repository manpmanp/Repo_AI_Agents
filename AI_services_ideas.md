## Long-Term Vision
Rather than choosing which AI service to build first, the Opportunity Discovery Engine should determine this automatically by continuously analyzing evidence from the market.
For every business function, the system should answer questions such as:
* Which business functions consume the most time?
* Which activities are repeated most frequently?
* Which tasks are still performed manually?
* Which operational problems occur most often?
* Which problems have the highest financial impact?
* Which problems affect the largest number of businesses?
* Which problems have weak or outdated existing solutions?
* Which problems can realistically be solved with AI?
* Which industries experience these problems most frequently?
* Which businesses would be willing to pay for a solution?
The highest-ranked opportunities become candidates for new AI products and services.
The ultimate goal is to build a continuously improving AI Opportunity Discovery Engine that identifies valuable business problems first, validates them with evidence, and only then recommends AI services that businesses are likely to purchase.
